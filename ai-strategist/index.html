Privacy Policy for AI Strategist

Effective Date: July 27, 2026

AI Strategist is an Instagram messaging and content automation application operated by Jo Moo Jin.

1. Information We Collect

When an Instagram account interacts with AI Strategist, the application may process information made available through the Meta and Instagram APIs, including:

  • Instagram user ID and username
  • Public profile information made available by Instagram
  • Direct messages, comments, message reactions, and related content
  • Message and event timestamps
  • Webhook event metadata
  • Technical logs required for security, troubleshooting, and service operation

2. How We Use Information

We use this information only to:

  • Receive and process Instagram messages and comments
  • Provide automated or assisted responses
  • Operate Instagram account management and customer-support functions
  • Diagnose errors and maintain service security
  • Prevent abuse, duplicate processing, and unauthorized requests
  • Improve the reliability of the application

We do not sell personal information or use it for unrelated advertising or profiling.

3. Sharing of Information

Information may be processed through Meta APIs and necessary infrastructure providers used to operate the application, including hosting, database, logging, and notification services.

Information is shared only when necessary to operate the service, comply with legal obligations, or protect the security of users and the application.

4. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, resolve technical or security issues, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, it is deleted or anonymized where reasonably possible.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including access controls, secret management, request-signature verification, and restricted operational access.

No system can guarantee absolute security.
